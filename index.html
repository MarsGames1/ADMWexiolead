Monthly Lead Allocation
How leads are distributed to clients each month

On the 1st of each month, every active subscriber's leads_used counter resets to 0 automatically.
Clients access their leads via the client panel — filtering, previewing and exporting within their monthly limit.
Curated packs are pre-assigned by segment and delivered as a separate allocation on top of self-service exports.
